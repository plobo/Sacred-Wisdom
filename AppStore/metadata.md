### Promotional text (170 max)
Explore what the Bhagavad Gita, Bible, Quran, Tanakh, Dhammapada and Guru Granth Sahib say, side by side, in one calm, offline library.

### Description
Sacred Wisdom brings passages from six traditions into one place: Hinduism, Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Buddhism and Sikhism. Every tradition gets the same space and the same care.

• Search all six collections at once, offline, by keyword or exact phrase, including Arabic and Hebrew without vowel marks.
• Read each passage in its original script (Sanskrit, Hebrew, Arabic, Pali, Gurmukhi) alongside one or more translations, with full source and license attribution.
• See related passages from other traditions, linked by clearly labeled editorial themes.
• Save passages, add private notes and organize your own collections.
• Ask a question and get passages from different traditions, quoted exactly. On supported devices, an optional on-device explanation is clearly labeled and always cites the passages it relies on.
• Use Siri and Shortcuts: "Ask Sacred Wisdom", "Find verses about peace in Sacred Wisdom", "What is today's passage in Sacred Wisdom".
• Search by voice, recognized on your device.
• Download complete chapters for offline reading.
• Light and Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, right-to-left scripts.

Sacred Wisdom never invents scripture. Every quotation comes from a verified, attributed source. Your bookmarks, notes, history and questions stay on your device.

## App Privacy (App Store Connect answers)

- **Data collection:** Data Not Collected. No analytics, no accounts, no tracking (matches `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`).
- **Network:** only to download content packages from the configured content server (no personal data sent).
- **Microphone / Speech Recognition:** used only while the user searches by voice; recognition runs on device.

## App Review notes

- No sign-in required. All content works offline.
- Voice search needs microphone and speech-recognition permission; it is on-device only.
- AI explanations use Apple's on-device Foundation Models (iOS 26+, Apple Intelligence enabled). Otherwise the app shows passages without an explanation. AI can be turned off in Settings.
- Offline Content: if `CONTENT_MANIFEST_URL` is not set for the build, the screen states that no content server is configured.

## Content rights

Every source and license is listed in the app under Settings → Sources & Licenses. **Confirm these before submission:**
- Tanzil Quran text: verbatim redistribution with attribution (the app never alters it).
- SuttaCentral Pali root text: digital edition license.
- BaniDB Gurmukhi text: database terms of use.
- King James Version: Crown rights in the United Kingdom (letters patent). This may affect UK distribution.
